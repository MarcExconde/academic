How the NBA Began

The National Basketball Association was originally founded in 1946 as the Basketball Association of America (BAA). It was created by major arena owners looking to fill open dates in their venues. In 1949, the BAA merged with the competing National Basketball League (NBL), officially giving birth to the modern NBA.

Founders & Key Figures

Maurice Podoloff served as the league's first president, laying the structural groundwork. Arena owners like Walter Brown (founder of the Boston Celtics) were instrumental in organizing inter-city basketball leagues into a mainstream national product.

Father of Basketball

Dr. James Naismith, a Canadian physical education instructor, invented basketball in December 1891 at the YMCA in Springfield, Massachusetts. Tasked with creating an indoor game to keep students active during harsh winters, he wrote the original 13 rules and hung two peach baskets as goals.
